# Code of conduct

This project serves every resident of Miami-Dade, whatever their party or views. Its spaces should work the same way.

## Expected
- Treat people with respect, including people you disagree with.
- Argue about the work: the code, the data, the sources.
- Assume good faith. Ask before you accuse.
- Accept correction, and correct with evidence.

## Not allowed
- Harassment, insults, threats, or slurs.
- Personal attacks on officials, candidates or contributors. Criticize the record, not the person.
- Campaigning, endorsing, or pushing a party or candidate in project spaces.
- Publishing anyone's private information, including addresses.
- Sexual content or unwelcome attention.

## Scope
Issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space where you represent the project.

## Enforcement
Report to conduct@wethepeople305.org. Reports are kept private. The maintainer may remove content, warn, or ban someone from the project, depending on what happened. Until the project has a board, the maintainer handles reports.

## Reference
This code is based on the ideas in the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
