Where the money comes from, and what Amendment 3 takes out

Florida voters passed Amendment 3 on November 3, 2026. It raises the homestead exemption on non-school property taxes to $150,000 in 2027 and $250,000 in 2028. This page shows the budget your elected officials now have to rework.

Where the county's money comes from

Most of the budget is not property tax. Fees paid at the airport, the seaport and on water bills stay with those services, so cutting them does not close the gap.

Which funds lose property tax

The county collects property tax into four separate funds. Each one takes its own hit and has to balance on its own.

What the Countywide General Fund pays for

Amounts are General Fund dollars only. Source: Appendix A.

Services with their own property tax

Fire Rescue and the Library each have a taxing district. Their loss comes out of their own budgets, not the General Fund.

Services that run on fees and grants

These get no General Fund money. Amendment 3 does not touch their revenue, and their money cannot be moved to cover the gap.

Source: Appendix A. Tourist taxes are hotel and restaurant taxes with uses set by state law.
