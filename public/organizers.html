Organizer admission

DRAFT. These criteria are waiting for the founder's approval and may change.

The organizer role is for organizations, not individuals, and it is granted by criteria, not by name. Anyone can be a citizen. An organization becomes an organizer when a person on our side checks its application against the criteria below and approves it. The same criteria apply to every organization, whatever its views.

Criteria

  1. The organization does civic work in Miami-Dade County: for example attending public meetings, public comment, voter education, or neighborhood service.
  2. The organization can be checked in public: a website, a public social media page, a Florida corporation record, or a listing of its public meetings.
  3. The organization is not a candidate, a campaign, a political party, or a political committee, and is not run by one.
  4. The organization has someone who can answer questions from the reviewer about this application.
  5. The organization agrees to the project Code of Conduct and to use organizer tools within it.

Full text and how review works: docs/organizer-criteria.md.
