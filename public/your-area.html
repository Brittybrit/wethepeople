Who works for you, and what is about to be decided

Put in your home address. You get every seat that represents it, from your city to Washington, the next meetings and decision points, and the most effective thing you can do now.

    As you type, the text goes to our server and on to the Esri geocoder to suggest addresses; your chosen address goes on to the Esri and U.S. Census geocoders and the county's precinct map to find your precinct. We do not log or keep it. Only the precinct number stays in the page link.

    Who works for you

    Every seat that represents this precinct, closest to home first. Money behind each official is on Ballot305.

    Appointed to run your government

    Not on any ballot. Hired or appointed by the officials above, and they run the day-to-day.

    Courts that hear your cases

    Circuit and county judges are elected. Appeals court and Supreme Court justices are appointed, then face retention votes.

    Boards that spend public money for you

    Countywide boards and authorities, mostly appointed, that tax, borrow or spend for everyone in Miami-Dade.
