What this is

Open-source civic intelligence: Our government works for us, not the other way around.

Personalized, local and actionable: the public resources you’re entitled to, the government decisions coming that will touch your life and your community, and the most effective thing you can do right now with what you’ve got.

Today it runs as Ballot305, the free voter guide for the November 3 election, with an open data API.
