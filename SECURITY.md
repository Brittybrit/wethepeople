# Security

## Report a vulnerability
Email security@wethepeople305.org. Do not open a public issue.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what an attacker could do with it. Do not include anyone's address or other personal data you came across; describe it instead.

## Scope
In scope:
- wethepeople305.org and www.wethepeople305.org: the site, `/api/v1/*` and `/mcp`.
- The code in this repository, including CI workflows, prompts and the model adapter.

Out of scope:
- Ballot305 (ballot305.org). Report to the same address.
- Esri, the U.S. Census geocoder, Miami-Dade GIS, Cloudflare, GitHub and model providers. Report to them.
- Volume attacks, spam, social engineering and physical attacks.
- Findings that need a compromised device or browser.

## What to expect
This is a one-person project.
- We acknowledge your report within 3 business days.
- We tell you whether we can reproduce it and what we plan to do.
- We fix high-impact issues first and tell you when the fix is live.
- We credit you in the fix commit if you want, by the name you give.

There is no bug bounty.

## Safe harbor
We will not pursue legal action against good-faith research that:
- stays within the scope above,
- does not access, keep or share other people's data,
- does not degrade the service for others (no load testing or bulk geocoding),
- gives us reasonable time to fix the issue before you disclose it.

If you are unsure whether something is allowed, ask first at the address above.
